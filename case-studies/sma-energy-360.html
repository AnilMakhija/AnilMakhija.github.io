SMA Energy and SMA 360°: connected-device commissioning on iOS

Two production iOS apps for SMA Solar Technology AG: a consumer energy application (SMA Energy) and an installer and commissioning application (SMA 360°). My work centred on BLE and Wi-Fi device workflows and the concurrency model behind them.

What the work involved

Commissioning a connected inverter means discovering it over BLE, getting it onto the site’s Wi-Fi, and keeping its firmware current. Installers often do this for several devices in a single visit, so the app has to manage multiple radio connections at once without corrupting shared state.

SMA 360° (installer app)

Installer and commissioning application for iPhone.

  • Multi-device discovery for up to five inverters in one session.
  • Wi-Fi provisioning performed through BLE characteristic writes.
  • OTA firmware update flows.
  • Concurrent multi-device BLE management built on Swift Concurrency: device state isolated in actors, with TaskGroup coordinating parallel operations.
  • Used Thread Sanitizer during testing to check for data races in concurrent device operations.
  • QR-based device onboarding and Sunny Portal registration flows.
